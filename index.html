How to read EMA 9 and EMA 21

EMA 9: reacts faster to recent price changes. It is the faster trend line.

EMA 21: reacts more slowly and helps show the broader short-term direction.

Possible bullish setup: EMA 9 crosses above EMA 21 and price holds above both. Wait for candle close and check risk; a crossover can fail.

Possible bearish setup: EMA 9 crosses below EMA 21 and price stays below both. Wait for candle close and check risk; a crossover can fail.

Neutral: lines are close, crossing repeatedly, or price is between them. Choppy conditions can produce false signals.
